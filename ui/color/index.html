Color section, ten ways

The same four inks and three actions in ten arrangements, each drawn in the dark rail at its real width. Hover the icon buttons for their labels.

Color–
1Inline

Swatches and the three actions share one row. Actions are icon buttons at the right, labelled on hover. One line, no chrome.

Color–
2Segmented

The three actions as one segmented control under the swatches, the way iOS groups peers. Equal weight, one outline.

3Strip

The palette drawn as one continuous bar of bands, in order, so it reads as a single object. Actions as quiet text links.

Color–
4List

Settings-style rows: swatch, role, hex, chevron. Every ink is a target you can read. Actions sit in a footer row.

Color–
5Header

Actions live in the section header beside the title, leaving the body to the swatches alone. The lightest body of the ten.

Color–
6Grouped

A macOS-style inset group: swatches on top, a hairline, then three borderless text actions in a row, like an action sheet.

Color–
7Menu

Swatches plus one ••• button. The three actions live in a menu, shown open here. Nothing competes with the colours.

Color–
8Roles

Rounded squares with the role each ink plays underneath, since order is meaning here. Actions as icons at the right.

Color–
9Chips

Each ink as a pill with its dot and hex code, wrapping as needed. Precise and copyable. Actions as icons.

Color–
10Primary

One clear action. A full-width New colors button carries the weight; Rotate and Save are small icons flanking the swatches.
