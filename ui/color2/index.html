Color, the simple way

One choice on top: Random or Mine. Below it, the swatches, and one verb that never changes its name: Shuffle. That's the whole section. Rotate, Edit and the on/off switch are gone; their jobs are covered by things people already understand.

The source is the choiceRandom is what the tool deals. Mine is what you saved. A segmented control makes it a fact you can see, not a switch you have to interpret.
Shuffle always shufflesOn Random it deals a new palette. On Mine it reorders your set. One word, one button, one place.
Swatches are the editorTap a swatch to change it, drag to reorder, + to add. On Mine, changes keep themselves. No Edit mode, no Save button to remember.
Save is a one-offSave as mine appears only on Random. It copies the current palette into Mine and switches you there. That's the only time you're asked to do anything.

If a segmented control is still too much, collapse it to a single Mine heart on the swatch row. Off: the tool's colours, and tapping the heart saves them as yours and lights up. On: your colours. Tapping again goes back to Random, with your set kept. Shuffle stays put underneath. This is the least UI possible, at the cost of hiding the "save" step inside a toggle, which some people will miss.
