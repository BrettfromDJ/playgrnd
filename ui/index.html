Pith ⁄ interface options

Five layouts, controls on the left and the artwork on the right. Each one is the working tool, not a picture of it — the sliders move and the artwork redraws. Nothing here is live on the site.

1DockOne column, one scroll. New at the top; Animate, Export and the two export buttons bolted to the foot so they never scroll away. The dials that go with them sit in the scroll, so the panel is not eaten by controls you are not using. 2TabsEvery group becomes a tab, Animate and Export among them, so nothing scrolls and each panel gets the full height. The three switches stay on show above the tabs. 3DenseControls two to a row so far more of the panel is in view at once, with the variation number set large on the New button and the switches along the top. 4RailA rail down the left picks the group and the panel beside it shows only that one, so nothing ever scrolls. Animate and Export are the last two entries on the rail and their switches sit under it, always on show. 5CardThe artwork runs to the edges of its half and the controls sit on a card over the left of it. New is a single wide action at the head, the switches and the two export buttons a fixed bar at the foot.
