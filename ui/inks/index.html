Color block · named colours + inks

Tokens, Oddgrid, Specimen, Stipple and Modular have one or two named colours (Ground, Grid, Paper, Accent) beside a row of inks. Five ways to lay that out, each in both states.

Named colours lead the row with their job written under them; a hairline separates them from the inks. Everything colour is one line.
